The meaning

Beyond the Bone

“The soul of a meal was never in the bone.”

For years we were told that comfort, depth and craving only come from one place. We wanted to find out what lies past it, so we built a kitchen on that question.

Our badge says it best: a bone, shaped entirely from carrots, kale and beets. It is the shape you know, made only of the earth. Every dish here follows the same idea: bold, silky, deeply savoury, and all of it plant-born.
